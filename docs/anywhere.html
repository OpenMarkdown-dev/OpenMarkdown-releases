FAQ

Can I use Anywhere now?

It’s in a private beta on macOS, and we’re letting people in a few at a time. Join the waitlist and we’ll send you an invite email when a spot opens. The app itself is free and needs no account: download it any time.

What is your refund policy?

Changed your mind? Ask within 7 days of paying for Anywhere+ and we refund you in full, no questions asked. Supporter and Founding Supporter backing is a one-time gift and isn’t refundable.

What do Supporters get?

Supporters ($10, one-time) get their name in our supporters file, a supporter badge and early development notes. Founding Supporters ($50, one-time) also get experimental builds before they ship, development updates with a direct line to us, and a say in where we take it. See the tiers.

Which AI apps can use my notes?

Claude (the desktop app and claude.ai), once you connect it to your account. It can read, search and add to the notes in your synced folder. ChatGPT and Claude Code come next; we’re testing them during the beta.

Do I need an account to use OpenMarkdown?

No. The app is free and works without an account. You only sign up to sync with Anywhere.
