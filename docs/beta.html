Getting started

  1. When the beta is ready, download itDownload the beta, open the disk image and drag OpenMarkdown into Applications. It replaces the OpenMarkdown you have now.
  2. Open OpenMarkdown, go to Settings → Anywhere and choose Sign in. The app opens a sign-in page in your browser: use the email address your invite went to. Signing in on this website doesn’t sign the app in.
  3. Choose the folder to sync. The free plan syncs one folder of about 100 notes and keeps 7 days of history.
  4. Connect your AI: see Connecting your AI below.

Connecting your AI

OpenMarkdown MCP lets an AI find, read and edit your notes. It comes in two versions, and we suggest adding both for the best experience.

Add OpenMarkdown (on your Mac)

  1. In OpenMarkdown, press ⌘⇧A to open the agent sidebar, and choose Chat apps.
  2. Choose Connect next to Claude or ChatGPT, and follow what it asks.

Claude Code: start it from the agent sidebar and the OpenMarkdown plugin installs itself. In your own terminal, run claude plugin marketplace add OpenMarkdown-dev/OpenMarkdown-releases and then claude plugin install openmarkdown@openmarkdown.

Add OpenMarkdown Cloud (online)

  1. On claude.ai, open Settings → Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it OpenMarkdown Cloud and paste https://mcp.openmarkdown.dev/mcp.
  3. Sign in with your invite email. It then shows up in the Claude phone app too.

About the beta

The beta is the same OpenMarkdown app with OpenMarkdown Anywhere turned on. Installing it replaces the OpenMarkdown in your Applications folder; your files and settings stay as they are, and it moves you to the regular release on its own once Anywhere is out for everyone.

What we’re testing this time:

  • Signing in to OpenMarkdown Anywhere from the app.
  • Syncing one folder, including between two Macs.
  • History for every note, and restoring an earlier version.
  • Reading and editing your notes from Claude and ChatGPT on your Mac, and from Claude on the web and on your phone.
  • Upgrading to Anywhere+.

Going back to the regular release

Download OpenMarkdown from the home page, open the disk image and drag OpenMarkdown into Applications, replacing the beta. Your files on disk are untouched. The regular app then gets regular updates again and doesn’t show Anywhere.

Telling us what broke
